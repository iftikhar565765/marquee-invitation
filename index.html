The Chronicle

Two hearts, one song, still playing

Our story begins on a rainy October night at a jazz bar neither of us meant to enter — one shared table when the room ran out, one record that refused to skip, and one conversation that outlasted the last tram home. He walked her to the station under one umbrella and exactly zero conversation about the weather; she texted him the name of the song before her train had left the platform.

Four years, two cities and a thousand playlists later, Alexander asked the question beneath the harbour lights with a ring hidden inside a book of Fitzgerald poems — and the city, for once, kept perfectly quiet. Nothing would mean more to us than to have you there when the band strikes up and the next chapter begins.
