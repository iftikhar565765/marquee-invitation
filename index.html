Our Story

Two hearts, written among the roses

Our story begins on a bright spring morning at the botanical gardens' rose festival — one shared bench beneath the pergola when the arbours ran out of shade, one paper cup of chai passed back and forth, and one conversation that outlasted every bloom on the trellis. He pointed out the rose named after a poet; she corrected him with the gardener's own catalogue, and neither of them noticed the afternoon slip away.

Four years, two cities and a balcony full of roses later, Alexander asked the question in the golden hour among the peonies, with the ring tucked into a bouquet he had grown himself — and the garden, for once, kept perfectly quiet. Nothing would mean more to us than to have you there when the garden gates open and the next chapter begins.
